Verdict: run these as diagnostic ablations; retain the incumbent unless evidence establishes transfer. Passing the stated local rule does not establish that.

1. **H4: coherent hypothesis, unjustified independence claim.** Selecting call identities independently does not make DE scores independent of bulk magnitudes, signs, variance, or downstream amplitude solving. Cheapest falsification: with matched donor draws and fixed call identities, compare emitted log2FC signs/magnitudes and DE components before versus after removing the preprocessing. Any DE deterioration falsifies “only costs MSE/PDS, without helping DE”; unchanged call identities alone prove nothing. Also inspect uncapped expression error. `eta0` is the right second arm: incumbent versus `eta0` isolates deflation removal with gating retained; `eta0` versus `dense` isolates gate removal without deflation. Add gate removal with deflation retained to complete the factorial and identify interaction. The existing three arms cannot do that.

2. **H5: conditionally well-posed, not established by the brief.** Both vectors must represent responses relative to the same control, on the same genes, log scale, and norm convention. Training in log-bulk space does not establish that the vector reaching the blend retains physical magnitude. A norm taken after per-target unit normalization contains no amplitude information; using the pre-normalization learned norm can be meaningful, but calibration remains unproven. Clean alternative: `response = 0.7 * pooled_raw_logFC + 0.3 * learned_raw_logFC`, without norm restoration. Trace the downstream amplitude solver: if it rescales to the same budget, it may erase the intended norm intervention. Report realized emitted magnitude, not just the substituted norm.

3. **No defensible numerical local threshold currently exists.** Neither +0.0017 nor exceeding the observed 0.074 discrepancy certifies transfer. A diagnostic build is worthwhile if it resolves a specific mechanism; replacement needs independent evidence connecting that mechanism’s local gains to official gains. Require matched controls, family-level uncertainty, component diagnostics, and untouched confirmation after SEARCH selection. `MSE > 0` is a conservative preregistered eligibility condition, but a poor universal improvement test: raw error can improve while the capped score remains exactly zero. Keep the registered pass/fail result; report raw/uncapped MSE separately. Any revised rule is prospective, not a rescue after seeing results.

4. **Ranked remaining experiments:**

   1. **Calibrate transfer using historical recipes with official scores.** Replay exact artifacts where possible; diagnose the known reversal by component, target composition, preprocessing, and emitter behavior. Test predictive performance on held-out recipes or recipe families. Another SEARCH optimization is lower value until this works.
   2. Complete H4’s missing factorial corner, if the first three arms leave an interaction unresolved.
   3. Test a norm-only intervention with direction and layout fixed, verifying that budget solving leaves a realized amplitude difference.

5. **I would refuse to sign off on local pass ⇒ final replacement.** Other problems: treating independently selected calls as independent DE outcomes; interpreting a norm substitution without checking downstream rescaling; treating 79 targets as independent despite family structure; and choosing among multiple SEARCH arms without selection-aware confirmation. Preserve frozen gates, including requested/native amplitude limits. Their passage establishes eligibility, not superiority. Diagnose the all-zero MSE component before interpreting it as evidence that expression accuracy cannot improve.

6. **The paired reversal disproves reliable ordering for this contrast; it does not calibrate a correction.** Moving incumbent → v5b gives local **+0.057116** but official validation **−0.01158**. Estimate the conditional distribution of **final official score change given local change, intervention family, and panel shift**—especially expected change and the probability of harm, or a predictive lower bound. One pair cannot estimate slope, residual variation, or intervention-specific transfer, and cannot justify subtracting a fixed discrepancy or inverting the surrogate. Multiple representative paired recipes, held-out predictive checks, and evidence addressing validation-to-final shift are needed. Thus neither +0.0017 nor hypothetical +0.08 alone justifies replacement. The latter warrants investigation, not promotion. This pair also does not prove every future local gain is useless.
