**Unanimous convergence:** the fix is a *learned linear map from donor response to target response in a
low-rank subspace*. Astra and Fable are nearly the same model (Astra adds explicit cell-state interaction
features; Fable adds the low-rank denoising projection that directly targets sign noise). Kimi's
target-specific variant is the same idea at per-target granularity, but 300 targets with few rows each is
data-poor on this panel.

## Decision
Run **`dirmap_1025`** — the Fable/Astra intersection, since it is the most concrete, most portable, and
cheapest to falsify:

1. Keep the incumbent frozen (pooling + MLP residual + 0.3 blend + renderer).
2. Add a learned ridge map `W` from PCA-reduced pooled donor response to the target line's response basis,
   rank k in {10,25,50}, selected by **inner leave-target-out CV on training families only**.
3. Add the mapped direction as a third component; use the official composite to choose whether it replaces
   or augments the pooled direction at a fixed, preregistered weight (no amplitude sweep).
4. Score with the validated local `cell_eval2` vcc2026 path; report all six components per fold.
5. 8 leave-one-family-out folds; the held-out family may contribute unperturbed controls only.

Promotion requires the intersection of the three kill tests:
- composite `score_avg` improves with a paired-bootstrap 95% CI excluding zero (Astra, Fable),
- improvement in >=5/8 families (Astra, Fable),
- **`direction_fidelity` improves by >=0.02 absolute** (all three; Kimi's +0.05 is reported separately),
- `sig_jaccard` does not decline (Astra), and
- the gain is not concentrated only in HepG2/RPE1 (Fable).
