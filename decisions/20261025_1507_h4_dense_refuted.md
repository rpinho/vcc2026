## What this means mechanistically

Fable's H4 predicted that the sign gate and 50-PC deflation had become pure cost, because the v9
layout now re-selects the call set from `confidence` independently. The measurement says the
opposite, and for a comprehensible reason:

1. **The gate is what makes the amplitude solver's job well-posed.** Removing it adds ~14.8k genes
   per target of small, noisy, non-consensus response. The budget solver then has to spread the
   same total across many more genes, so alpha drops (0.444 → 0.256) and each retained gene's
   amplitude shrinks. B/capped falls. That is the "undershoot" failure mode the gates exist to
   catch, and it is exactly what `G3_native_actual` flagged.
2. **PDS falls because the extra genes are noise.** PDS is a correlation over the bulk profile;
   adding uncorrelated genes to the profile dilutes it. -0.041 is large and unambiguous.
3. **Reach collapses (-0.023)** because the call ordering is now keyed on an ungated response,
   where the sign is dominated by small noisy values rather than cross-source agreement.

So the gate is not vestigial. It is doing two jobs at once: constraining which genes the amplitude
budget is spread over, and providing a well-founded sign for the call ordering. Kimi's caveat was
right — "independent selection of call support does not imply independence of DE log2FC, signs,
confidence ordering, or the amplitude/budget solution." Astra's was sharper still: the cheapest
falsification was to check whether the *DE inputs* move, and they did.

## Caveat on the comparison

`dense` necessarily uses a different confidence definition from the others, because there is no
gate to key on (`confidence_policy: ungated_undeflated_preblend_prealpha_response`, recorded in
its receipt). That is a genuine confound: part of the -0.011 could be the confidence change rather
than the profile change. This does not rescue H4 — the *expression* side (PDS, raw u, alpha, B) is
independent of the confidence array and it also got worse — but the DE components should be read
as "profile + ordering changed together", not as a clean isolation.

The `eta0` arm now running removes only the deflation and keeps the gate, which separates the two
half-mechanisms cleanly. If `eta0` is also worse, the gate and the deflation are both load-bearing
and H4 is closed on both halves.
