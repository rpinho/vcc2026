**1. If the manifold does not beat B0: run Astra's response-reliability source weighting Monday. Sidechain waits.**
Reason: if the manifold loses, the diagnosis is that "which donors you transfer from" chosen by STRING similarity is not better than what B0 already does. Sidechain staged progression is more source-selection machinery stacked on the same premise that just failed — it is doubling down. Reliability weighting asks a different question (is the problem donor *noise*, not donor *choice*), and it's a one-day build: compute split-half reproducibility per donor response on the 168, weight the pooled/manifold estimate by it, predict the 79. Protocol: fit on 168 only, including any weight temperature chosen by CV on the 168. **2. If the manifold does beat B0: four things must be true before anyone builds a package.**
(i) B2 > B0 with 95% CI excluding zero on the official composite, and the gain sits in the expression-MSE component — if it's winning on DE, the gap decomposition says that won't transfer. (ii) B2 > B5. B5 is the cheap competitor: if shrinking B0 toward the training mean gets you the same points, the manifold is an expensive way of doing shrinkage and you ship B5. (iii) The gain is not concentrated in the low-cell tertile (62-cell truth is noisy; a method that "wins" there is fitting noise). (iv) Nothing in F3's hyperparameters was chosen with the 79 in view — including looking at a prior leaderboard score on those 79. That last one is the first invalidator: if any bandwidth/shrinkage constant was tuned against a submission scored on this panel, the out-of-sample claim is false and the result is a leak. Second invalidator: B5 ≈ B2. The package, if built, is B2 plus one calibration scalar fit by CV on the 168 — not a new architecture, not a STRING ensemble.

**3. B0 below chance: one-hour triage, not an investigation — and h2h2 already contains the triage.**
Below chance across 79 targets is not what a no-skill model looks like; no-skill is *at* chance. Systematically below is an artifact signature: control mismatch (B0's baseline differs from the ctrl used in truth reconstruction), gene-index misalignment between inference_H1.npz and the bank, or a global offset flipping signs on near-zero predictions. Check: read B3 and B4's direction fidelity from the same h2h2 output. If only B0 is below chance, it's real, and then ask the only question that matters: is direction a term in the official scorer? If it isn't, stop — it doesn't touch the objective and we're matched to top-100 on the DE terms that do. If it is, the discrepancy with our official DE standing must be reconciled before we trust the gap decomposition. As to what carries our score: on the decomposition's own numbers, it's a mid-pack expression term and median DE overlap. That's consistent with a model that gets *which* genes move without getting *how far or which way*. Not a mystery, not a bug hunt.
