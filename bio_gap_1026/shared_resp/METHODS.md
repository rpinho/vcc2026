This is an analysis of the 168 retained H1 training perturbations, with no 79-target input or outcome opened. The baseline archive and per-target CSV are read from the preceding job without importing its runtime or executing its reconstruction, which would write outside this directory. The retained B0 is the preceding job's integer-emitted pseudobulk. Its E=1.642331892973409, mean cosine=0.084134974165276 and PDS=0.8558953521528372 reproduce that job. Input hashes are in results/analysis.json; the verifier rehashes them.

All vectors use the preceding job's 18,077 measured genes and log1p(CP50k) pseudobulk deltas. We zero each perturbation's own output gene before fitting, shared-mean construction and E/cosine evaluation, following ../analyze.py. A shared mean applied to target j also has gene j zeroed. Other perturbation genes remain in the E/cosine axis. Thus these are the preceding job's own-gene-excluded deltas, not knockdown-inclusive means. PDS instead excludes every target gene in the entire fixed 168-target panel, as the installed vcc2026 preset requires.

The primary table evaluates the requested vector equations directly. This makes (a) exactly the unscaled LOO mean, (b) exactly m + beta P, and (c) exactly m + beta (P - proj_m P). It avoids silently altering the stipulated shared-response baseline with a nonlinear feasibility map. A second full table in results/analysis.json and results/projected_table.md applies the previous job's clipped, renormalized molecule-probability projection to every challenger. The previous family arm is reproduced after that projection: E=1.469451337266440, PDS=0.8597447961220416 and B/B0=0.803506076184390. The primary raw-vector version is E=1.468417625073367, PDS=0.8595309381237525 and B/B0=0.802253682535668. This small convention difference does not change the reading. Neither table renders cells/counts, computes DE, packages anything, or estimates official MSE.

For an outer evaluation target t, S contains the other 167 targets. To fit beta_b, each j in S receives m(S without j), and beta minimizes summed gene-space squared residual against Y_j - m(S without j), using the fixed B0_j predictor. For beta_c, replace B0_j with its component orthogonal to that inner mean. Fit one unrestricted scalar through the residual origin; the coefficient on the shared mean is fixed at one as requested. No hyperparameter is selected. The outer prediction uses mean(S) and the fitted scalar. The outer beta ranges are 0.11343–0.12442 for (b), and 0.10540–0.11510 for (c); means are 0.12146 and 0.11278. All coefficient fits exclude the outer target entirely.

The source-family features reproduce ../reconstruct.py and ../analyze.py: average measured source responses within seven non-H1 families, realize their continuous pseudobulk, round the saved feature precision to float32 as in the preceding archive, and zero the own target gene. An absent donor family contributes zero residual from B0. Thus R_fj = F_fj - B0_j, or zero if missing. No H1 source bank is read; only matching training168 rows in the other source banks enter calculations. Source-bank hashes match the manifest.

Arm (d) is B0 plus the preceding job's source-family residual ridge, with gram = sum over S of R_j R_j transpose, and fixed ridge penalty trace(gram)/7. Arm (e) adds the same fixed source-family feature basis to (c), but refits its ridge against cross-fitted residuals from (c). For each j in the outer fitting set, that base prediction uses a shared mean without t and j, and a beta trained without t and j. Each training row k used to fit that beta in turn has a shared mean without t, j and k. Consequently the stacked correction never sees the outer held-out truth, including indirectly through any inner mean or coefficient. The family penalty is identical to the preceding job and is not tuned. Shared means and scalar fits are computed from small Gram cross-products for speed; an independent verifier checks every outer scalar and all 14,028 distinct inner pairs from explicitly selected subcohorts, plus selected actual gene-space fits. All family fits are independently replayed in gene space.

The projection-energy fraction is sum_t dot(Y_t,m_-t)^2 / ||m_-t||^2, divided by sum_t ||Y_t||^2. It is a descriptive per-target projection oracle: the held-out truth determines the amplitude only for this diagnostic, never for an arm. The mean baseline's 1-E is a different quantity and uses fixed amplitude one. Orthogonal decomposition uses each target's own cross-fitted m in both P and Y. We report unscaled orthogonal E and cosine, and a separately labeled nested-scaled orthogonal E in results/verification.json.

E is a ratio of summed SSE to summed truth energy; cosine is an unweighted mean over targets. B is sum_t ||prediction_t - across-target mean(prediction)||^2; dividing by B0's B removes any choice of sum versus mean convention. Centering is descriptive and does not feed fitting. The own-gene masking and LOO means induce the tiny nonzero B of arm (a). It is essentially a constant predictor.

PDS uses cosine against all 168 truth candidates, panel-wide target-gene exclusion, n-1 rank normalization and midrank ties, exactly as ../REPORT.md's 0.856 result. results/verification.json checks all 12 table entries with the installed cell_eval2.metrics.discrimination.discrimination_score using EvalConfig.from_preset('vcc2026'). PDS here is a training-panel pseudobulk comparator, not the full competition's six-metric evaluation.

The 2000 paired target bootstrap replicates use seed 20261026. Each replicate resamples 168 target indices with replacement and recomputes the ratio-of-sums E and mean per-target PDS. The same indices serve every arm, so reported differences are paired. Percentile 95% CIs are exploratory and condition on the fitted LOO predictions and fixed 168-candidate retrieval panel. We do not refit nested models or duplicate candidates inside bootstrap replicates. This follows the preceding job's bootstrap estimand and does not establish uncertainty in a new destination cell type. Full arm/absolute intervals and differences are retained in results/analysis.json; the report displays difference intervals versus B0.

The independent verifier does not import analyze.py. It rehashes inputs; recomputes the full nested coefficient set; reconstructs source features; independently replays all family fits; recomputes per-target losses, native PDS, spread and paired bootstrap intervals; and certifies full-coordinate SSE for every primary arm by exact integer/dyadic-rational arithmetic on its independently replayed float64 vectors. This numerical certificate is not a certificate of official MSE or biological improvement.
