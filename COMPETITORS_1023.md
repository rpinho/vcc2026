## What actually changed since October 19

1. **Sidechain supplies a clear progression, not an exact public winning bundle.** Its author notes report SER-21 0.1757 → SER-27 0.1875 by changing bulk strength while the other components approximately held. SER-29 adds five source types and per-context bulk strength, reaching 0.2226 with MSE 0.212 → 0.299. The added source types are CD4, another H1 screen, pooled stem-cell screens, Calu-3 and glioblastoma. Multiple factors changed together at SER-29; its gain cannot be assigned entirely to source count or forecast for us. [Primary notes](https://saberhq.com/sidechain/models/).
2. **A new independent source-only export documents the same mean-versus-distribution problem.** Model4's diagnostics distinguish accurate moments from detection/DE recovery. Its balanced-sampling gain was below its own promotion threshold, and the preserving emitter missed many injected increases. This reinforces the need to measure actual counts and null behavior, not reopen our failed selective-scatter candidate. [Source and results](https://github.com/FupaulHuang/virtual-cell-model4).
3. **Evaluation and cross-context-transfer papers have new October material.** They mostly support a better experiment design or a small comparator, not immediate architecture replacement. Details below.

## Best three next moves

1. **Repair the evidence before selecting another package.** Specify exact billmA/v9a/v9b/slim replay contracts, a held-context/control allocation test inspired by Refara, negative controls and complete-panel official scoring. Keep the last safety entry unchanged. A correct metric implementation alone does not solve representativeness.
2. **Run one factor-isolated source/amplitude comparison only after move 1 is usable.** Separate the billmB sampling/mask/source changes, inspect full-source centering and matched controls, and test Sidechain's bulk-strength idea with the original emitter. Do not transfer A/B/C-specific tuning into D/E/F by assertion.
3. **Build one modest independent controls-only estimator comparator.** Start with the sigRecon protocol/package in an isolated R environment, retain our incumbent integer boundary, and compare raw/scaled six-metric results with explicit gene/magnitude contracts. If it cannot produce a defensible full response profile, retain it as a diagnostic and stop before packaging.
