It could fail because:

- **Coverage can make the opportunity small.** A good bank covering a handful of targets cannot rescue the whole panel. For MSE, those targets’ response energy also matters.
- **Baseline-expression similarity does not establish response similarity.** Controls can identify the line without identifying how it responds.
- **Routing can discard useful evidence.** Prefer same-line evidence where independently supported; retain the existing fallback elsewhere.
