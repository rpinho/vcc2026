## 5. Relevant to us (ranked)

1. **T3V200 "P1a" magnitude lookup table, an amplitude calibration fitted on public truth DE.**
   It calibrates predicted DE magnitudes by direction-rank × baseline-expression bins, fitted on
   five public truth contexts and never on target labels, and it is deployable on D/E/F controls
   alone. This is a recipe for amplitude calibration. The validation gap between "Res" and us is
   mostly in MSE: scaled MSE 0.391 vs our billmA 0.061, against scaled PDS 0.801 vs 0.706, so MSE
   accounts for about 0.055 of the 0.097 overall gap.
   https://github.com/Ji-Yuanlan/T3V200_final (docs/preparation_and_fitting.md, docs/method.md)
2. **T3V200 "P1N" adjustment for non-called genes.** A 2×5 table with λ 0.8 for genes that are not
   called. With PDS and MSE unchanged, it improved NMAE and reach (+0.0016 overall, R7 → R8). This is
   a cheap lever for DE fidelity and calibration outside the called set.
   https://github.com/Ji-Yuanlan/T3V200_final/blob/main/docs/results.md
3. **Centered-source sign and magnitude-rank transfer with isotonic rendering.** Centering an
   independent source on known targets raised PDS on every fold. Transferring signs and ranks
   rather than raw magnitudes, then rendering with isotonic regression, gave DES above the 2025
   champion. This is directly relevant to sign precision and to preserving PDS, and it bears on the
   full-pool-centering lever (T2). https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.10.20.760514
4. **T3V200 "SRB": boost the PC6–50 component of strong rows (in target-control PC space) while
   keeping row energy.** This is a direction tweak aimed at PDS / discrimination that leaves
   amplitude unchanged. Its own A/B effect is not isolated in their scores.
   https://github.com/Ji-Yuanlan/T3V200_final/blob/main/docs/method.md
5. **Gating lesson from T3V200's D/E/F run.** Its cross-fit gate (0.8) reverted context D to base
   counts, so a calibration layer was deployed per context only where it passed. This is a working
   example of per-context fallback for the final panel vcc2026-test-1.
   https://github.com/Ji-Yuanlan/T3V200_final/blob/main/evidence/final_submission.json
6. **Shared-clone inflation of split-half ceilings.** Any local "from-replicate" anchor or
   reliability weight built from random cell halves may be inflated, most of all for primary
   T-cell (CD4) sources. https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32962
7. **Hidden final-phase activity is slowing.** 6–26 submissions per 6 h on 10-25 and 10-26, against
   45–92 on 10-22. Most top-8 teams show no D/E/F resubmission at all. This is context only and
   gives no score information. `competitors/raw/lb-2026-10-26T130923Z.json`

Licence caveat: T3V200 has **no licence**, so its ideas can be re-implemented independently, but
its code must not be copied. The 760514 paper is CC-BY and abstract-only here.
